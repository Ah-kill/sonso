Why SoNSo Start

Good ideas shouldn't stall at the first step.

Many startups stop before they begin, not because the idea is weak, but because there's no one to build it and agency prices are out of reach on day one. SoNSo Start keeps the upfront cost low and takes a small share of the company instead, so we only do well if you do.

How it works

From idea to launched product, step by step.

  1. Idea call

    A free call. Tell us the idea, who it's for and what you've done so far. We'll tell you honestly whether it's a fit for SoNSo Start.

  2. Scoping

    Together we decide what the first version must do and what can wait. That becomes a fixed scope, so both sides know exactly what's being built.

  3. Proposal

    A written plan with the scope, timeline, the one-time fee and the stake terms, before anything is agreed.

  4. Agreement

    Everything goes into a proper written agreement. We encourage you to have your own adviser review it.

  5. Build in phases

    We build one phase at a time and show you working progress at each step, so you're never waiting months to see anything.

  6. Launch and support

    We launch with tracking and a dashboard in place, then support the product for an agreed period after launch.

  7. Your choice after that

    Continue with SoNSo on regular terms, or take the product forward with your own team. Either way, your company owns everything we built.

The deal

Low upfront cost. A small stake, earned fairly.

We want founders to know exactly what they're agreeing to before the first call. Here's how SoNSo Start works.

A minimal one-time fee

Far below our regular project pricing, because you're at the start. The amount depends on what we build and is agreed in writing.

A stake sized to the work

Our stake reflects the value of the work we discount, not a guess about what your idea is worth. You'll see exactly how it's worked out in the proposal.

Earned in stages

We earn the stake as we deliver: part when we sign, part at launch and the rest after the support period. If we don't deliver, we don't earn it.

Your right to buy it back

You can buy our stake back at a price agreed upfront, within a set period. If the company takes off, you're not locked into us for ever.

No valuation fights on day one

Where it suits both sides, our stake converts at your first funding round instead, so nobody has to put a price on an idea today.

Always a small minority

We never take control. You keep the majority of your company and the final say on every decision.

What's yours

Your company. Your product. Your decisions.

  • The majority and control of your company. Our stake is small and never comes with control.
  • Ownership of the product. The code, designs, content and data belong to your company.
  • Full access. Admin access to every account, tool and server we set up is in your company's name from day one.
  • A proper handover. Source code, logins and documentation are handed over, so another team could take it on.
  • Freedom to raise money. Our terms are written to be easy for future investors to understand, and we won't stand in the way of your fundraising.
  • Freedom to choose. After the support period there's no obligation to keep working with us.

Prepare for the call

Bring these, and we'll get much further in 30 minutes.

  • The problem, in one sentence. What's broken for whom?
  • Your first customers. Who will pay first, and how will you reach them?
  • What you've done so far. Conversations, sign-ups, sketches, a waiting list, anything.
  • What the first version must do. The three or four things it can't launch without.
  • Your timing and budget. When you need to launch, and roughly what you can put in as the one-time fee.
  • Your company status. Registered already, or not yet.

Don't have all of it? Book anyway. Working this out together is part of the first call.

Questions founders ask about SoNSo Start

Do you invest money in my startup?

No. We don't give cash. We contribute by building your product and systems, for a reduced one-time fee and a small stake.

How is the stake worked out?

It's tied to the value of the work we're discounting. The proposal shows the calculation, so you can see the stake is fair rather than a number we picked.

When do you actually get the stake?

In stages, as we deliver: part at signing, part at launch and the rest after the support period. If the project stops early, we only keep what we've earned.

Can I buy your stake back later?

Yes. The agreement includes a buyback right at a price agreed upfront, within a set period. Many founders use it once they raise money or start earning.

Will your stake make it harder to raise money later?

We write our terms to be simple and investor-friendly. Where it suits you, the stake can convert at your first funding round, which investors are used to seeing.

How much is the one-time fee?

It depends on the scope of your first version. It's far below our regular pricing, and we agree the exact amount in writing before work starts.

What if my company isn't registered yet?

That's common at this stage. We'll agree in writing how the stake is handled once the company is formed, before any work starts.

Who owns the product and the code?

Your company does. Code, designs, data and every account are yours, and we hand over everything at the end.

What if I want to add features during the build?

Good ideas are welcome. Anything outside the agreed scope goes into a later phase or is quoted separately at regular rates, so your first version still launches on time.

What if I decide to stop or pivot?

Tell us early. We keep only the part of the stake we've earned up to that point, and we'll talk through whether the work done can support the new direction.

Will you keep my idea confidential?

Yes. Everything you share stays confidential, and we're happy to sign an NDA before you go into detail.

Should I get my own legal advice?

Yes, we recommend it. Shares issued for services have legal and tax implications for both sides, so it's worth having your own adviser review the agreement.

What happens after launch?

We support the product for the agreed period. After that you can keep working with SoNSo on regular terms for growth, automation and reporting, or take it forward with your own team.
