About

We exist because four vendors can't build one system.

Growing businesses usually hire a web developer, an ad agency, a CRM consultant and someone to do the reports. Each does their part well enough, and nobody owns the whole chain. SoNSo is one team that does.

Why SoNSo exists

The gaps are between the vendors.

The expensive problems in a growing business rarely sit inside one tool. They sit in the gaps: the lead that never reached the CRM, the follow-up nobody sent, the ad that looked good on clicks and never produced a sale.

We build, automate, grow and measure as one connected system. An ad click lands on a page we built, the lead flows into a CRM we set up, follow-up happens automatically, and management sees cost per lead through to closed revenue on one dashboard.
