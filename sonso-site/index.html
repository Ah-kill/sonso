Connected by design

Most businesses hire four vendors who never talk to each other.

A web developer, an ad agency, a CRM consultant and someone who does the reports. Each does their part, and nobody can tell you which ad produced which sale. Here is one lead's journey when a single team builds the whole chain.

  1. Grow

    Ad click

    A prospect clicks your Google or Meta ad. The click is tracked with its campaign.

  2. Build

    Landing page

    They land on a page built for that ad and fill in a short form.

  3. Automate

    CRM

    The lead appears in your CRM with its source attached. Nobody copies anything.

  4. Automate

    Automated follow-up

    A WhatsApp message and email go out straight away, and a sales rep gets the task.

  5. Measure

    Dashboard

    Management sees cost per lead through to closed revenue, by campaign.

With four vendorsThe chain breaks at every handover. Leads sit in inboxes, follow-ups depend on memory, and the ad report shows clicks.

With SoNSoOne system, one set of data. You can trace any sale back to the ad that started it, and see which spend to increase.
