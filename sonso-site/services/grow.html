FAQs

Questions about performance marketing

Is ad spend included in your fee?

No. You pay ad spend directly to Meta, Google, YouTube or LinkedIn. Our fee is a monthly retainer for running and improving the campaigns.

What's in the free ads account review?

We look at your current campaign structure, tracking and spend, and tell you what we would change. You keep the findings whether or not you work with us.

How much should we spend on ads?

It depends on your market, margins and goals. We recommend a starting budget after the account review, and adjust it once the data shows what a qualified lead costs you.

Which platforms do you run?

Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google Search, YouTube and LinkedIn. We pick the mix based on where your buyers are.

Who owns the ad accounts?

Your business does. Campaigns run in accounts registered to you, and you keep full access and history if we stop working together.

When will we see results?

Tracking comes first, and campaigns need time to gather enough data to optimise. We agree review checkpoints at the start, so you know when to expect a clear read.
