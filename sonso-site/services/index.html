How a complete system comes together

  1. Process mapping

    A free call, then a working session where we map your business from first enquiry to repeat customer, including every tool and handover.

  2. System blueprint

    A written plan showing what gets built at each stage, how the pieces connect, the order of phases, and the scope and cost of each.

  3. Build in phases

    We build one phase at a time, starting with whatever saves the most time or brings in the most revenue. Each phase works on its own.

  4. Run and improve

    Once it's live, we run the campaigns, maintain the systems and review the numbers with you every month.
