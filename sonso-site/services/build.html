FAQs

Questions about websites

How long does a website take?

It depends on scope. A campaign landing page is a much smaller job than a multi-page site with a customer portal. You get a fixed timeline in writing before any work starts.

How do you price a website?

As a fixed-scope project. After the discovery call we send a written quote covering pages, features and timeline. Care plans are a separate monthly fee, and they're optional.

Which platform will you build on?

We recommend a platform during scoping, based on what the site needs to do and who will maintain it. We explain the trade-offs before you decide.

Will my team be able to edit the site?

We agree how content will be edited during scoping. Either your team edits it directly, or we make changes for you under a care plan.

Who owns the website and domain?

Your business does. The domain, hosting account and content are registered in your name.

Can you redesign our existing website?

Yes. We start by reviewing what your current site does well and where visitors drop off, then decide what to keep and what to rebuild.

Tell us what your website needs to do.

Share a few details and we'll come back with a fixed-scope quote, or book a call to talk it through first.
