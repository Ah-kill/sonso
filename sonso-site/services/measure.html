Which tools do you build dashboards in?

Mainly Microsoft Power BI and Google Looker Studio. We recommend one based on the systems and licences you already have.

Our data is messy. Can you still help?

Yes. Cleaning data and agreeing on definitions is part of every project. It's usually the step that makes the biggest difference.

Do we need a data warehouse?

Not always. Many businesses start by connecting their existing systems directly. We recommend a warehouse only when the volume or number of sources needs one.

How do you price a dashboard project?

As a fixed-scope project, agreed after the KPI workshop. Ongoing monthly reporting is optional and priced separately.

Who can see our data?

Only people you approve. Dashboards and data sources sit in accounts your business owns, and access is set per person.

How long does a dashboard take?

It depends on how many systems are involved and how clean the data is. You get a fixed timeline after the KPI workshop, before the build starts.
