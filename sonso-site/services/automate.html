Beyond the CRM

Automations that run the rest of the business

A CRM is usually the first step. Once your leads and customers live in one place, the same setup can take over the routine work around them. Start with one, or let the audit show which saves the most time.

Customer journeys

Every customer gets the right message at the right time.

Example: Booking confirmed, a reminder the day before, directions on the day and a feedback request afterwards.

Payments and collections

Get paid without chasing.

Example: Payment links sent with every quote, overdue invoices reminded on day 3, 7 and 14, and payments synced to Tally or Zoho Books.

Quotes and documents

Paperwork that writes itself.

Example: Quotes, proposals, contracts and certificates generated from templates with the customer's details filled in.

Operations and approvals

Routine decisions that don't wait for someone to remember.

Example: Low-stock alerts, draft purchase orders and approval requests sent to the right person on WhatsApp.

Customer support

No question left waiting.

Example: WhatsApp and email messages become tickets, common questions get an instant answer, and anything open too long goes to a manager.

Team and HR

Internal admin that runs itself.

Example: New-joiner checklists, leave approvals and daily task lists sent automatically.

Owner alerts

Your key numbers come to you.

Example: Yesterday's sales, collections and pending follow-ups on WhatsApp every morning. Full dashboards are part of Measure.

Marketing follow-through

Marketing that keeps working after the sale.

Example: Abandoned-cart reminders, review requests after a delivery, and win-back messages for customers who've gone quiet. Ad tracking is part of Grow.

Not sure which of these fits your business? The automation audit maps how work moves today and ranks what to automate first.

What happens in an automation audit?

We map how leads, orders and information move through your business today, list the tools involved, and mark every manual step. You get a written, prioritised list of what to automate and what it would save.

Do you only automate things inside the CRM?

No. Payments, documents, approvals, support, internal admin and reporting can all be automated, with or without a CRM. The audit shows which ones will save your team the most time.

Which CRM do you work with?

We set up and customise established CRMs such as Zoho, and build a custom CRM when your sales process needs one. We recommend one after the audit, based on how your team sells.

Can you automate WhatsApp messages?

Yes. We use the official WhatsApp Business platform, so messages are sent from your verified business number.

How do you price automation work?

An audit first, then a fixed-scope project, then an optional monthly support retainer. Prices are agreed after the audit, once the scope is clear.

Who owns the systems and data?

Your business does. Tools and accounts are set up in your name, and your data stays in systems you control.

Will my team need training?

Yes, and it's included. Every handover covers how to use the new setup and what to do when something looks wrong.
