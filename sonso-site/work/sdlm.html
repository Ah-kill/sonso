SDLM: the full chain from lead to dashboard.

A hospitality and franchise brand that needed leads followed up quickly and a clear view of what each lead cost and produced.

Client
SDLM
Industry
Hospitality and franchising
Stages
Automate · Grow · Measure

The problem

Franchise and hospitality enquiries arrive from ads, calls and messages, and they go cold fast. When leads, calls and reports live in separate tools, it's hard to answer simple questions: how quickly do we respond, what does a qualified lead cost, and where is the time going?

What SoNSo did

Grow

Ad campaigns feeding a lead flow directly into the CRM, so no lead waits in an inbox.

Automate

WhatsApp and email automation for follow-up. Calling integrated through APIs, with AI-written call summaries saved against each lead. AI-powered workflows, and a tech audit to decide what to automate first.

Measure

Management and KPI dashboards, reporting, data integration across systems, forecasting and performance analysis.

Results

The engagement is measured on three numbers:

  • Lead response time, from enquiry to first contact
  • Cost per qualified lead, not just cost per lead
  • Reporting time saved by automating dashboards and reports

Why it worked as one system

This is the connected model end to end: an ad produces a lead, the lead lands in the CRM, follow-up happens automatically, the call is summarised, and management sees the cost and outcome on one dashboard.
